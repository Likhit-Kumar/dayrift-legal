Questions

Is Dayrift free?

Yes. The free tier covers 50 tasks, 5 habits, AI scheduling, focus sessions and streaks. Premium removes the limits. Deleting a task or habit frees the slot back up.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet. Dayrift is Android only for now.

What does the AI actually do?

It reads your calendar, your habits and when you've historically got things done, then places unscheduled tasks into slots that fit. If you miss something, it moves it rather than marking it failed.

Do I have to use the AI companion?

No. Hailey is off by default and lives entirely in Settings. Dayrift works as a planner and habit tracker without her.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, at any time, from inside the app or via the account deletion page linked below.
