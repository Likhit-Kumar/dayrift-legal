Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your Day Rift account and all associated data. We're sorry to see you go.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Day Rift account and associated data using one of the following methods:

1 In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open the Day Rift app
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Scroll down to Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm your decision
2 Email Request

Send an email to request account deletion:

  • Email: dayrift.app@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include: Your registered email address and the reason for deletion (optional)
Send Deletion Request

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

Data Type Description Deletion Timeline
Account Information Name, email, profile picture, preferences Within 30 days
Habit Data All habits, tracking history, streaks Within 30 days
Workout Data Exercise logs, workout sessions, fitness records Within 30 days
Focus Sessions All focus session history and statistics Within 30 days
Goals & Quests Personal goals, milestones, achievements Within 30 days
Analytics Data Usage statistics, progress data Within 30 days

Data We May Retain

Certain data may be retained for legal or business purposes:

Data Type Reason for Retention Retention Period
Transaction Records Legal and tax compliance Up to 7 years
Support Communications Legal compliance and dispute resolution Up to 3 years
Anonymized Analytics Service improvement (cannot identify you) Indefinitely

Important Information

Warning: Account deletion is permanent

Once your account is deleted, all your data including habits, workouts, achievements, and progress history will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Cancel Subscription First

If you have an active premium subscription, please cancel it through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store before requesting account deletion. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within:

  • In-App: Immediate deletion initiation, complete within 30 days
  • Email: Response within 5 business days, deletion within 30 days of confirmation

Verification

For email requests, we may need to verify your identity to protect your account. Please use the email address associated with your Day Rift account when submitting your request.

Contact Us
